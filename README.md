# Virtual Business Twin

Virtual Business Twin is a financial decision-intelligence and simulation platform for individuals and businesses.

It is designed to maintain a governed financial state, evaluate proposed decisions, simulate multiple scenarios, stress-test resilience, map external factors into financial impact, track decisions over time, and explain results without presenting unjustified certainty as fact.

## Current product scope

- Personal Financial Twin
- Business Financial Twin
- Financial dashboard and health overview
- Decision simulator
- Base / best / worst / stress / custom scenarios
- What-if analysis
- Sensitivity analysis
- Stress testing
- Risk and resilience scoring
- Financial timeline projections
- Financial goals and goal-impact simulation
- Decision history and monitoring
- External-factor impact mapping
- AI-assisted financial explanations
- Enterprise/API foundation

## Architecture principle

The numerical engine owns calculations, scenario outputs, risk metrics and probabilities. AI is used for explanation, interpretation and insight generation; it must not invent probabilities or unsupported causal claims.
